CONNECTED OPERATIONS

See the business as one operating system.

Inventory, backlog, purchasing, scheduling, workforce and capacity affect one another. Arvenix is designed to connect those decisions instead of analyzing each function in isolation.

Inventory Management Improve turns, allocation, availability and working capital. Purchasing Optimization Use demand, inventory and open orders to improve replenishment. Backlog Management Turn sold work into a prioritized queue based on readiness and risk. Scheduling Optimization Match ready work with available field resources. Capacity Planning Understand where production capability is limiting growth. Workforce Planning Translate future demand into resource requirements by market. Operational Analytics Connect operating KPIs to revenue, EBITDA and working capital. Operational ROI Quantify potential EBITDA improvement, cash release and payback.
