The operating problem

Purchasing gets expensive when buyers cannot see consumption, committed demand and inbound supply together. Arvenix is designed to combine those signals and recommend whether to order, transfer, hold or cancel.

Consumption

Rolling usage, frequency, variability, seasonality and demand pattern.

Supply

Open POs, expected dates, supplier lead time, MOQ and receiving performance.

Requirement

Available inventory plus sold-job demand and projected near-term consumption.
