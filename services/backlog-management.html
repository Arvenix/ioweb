The operating problem

A backlog count does not tell management which jobs can move, which jobs are blocked, or where revenue is at risk. Arvenix is designed to rank sold jobs by readiness, aging, material position, technician capacity and customer constraints.

Job readiness

Material availability, contract status, customer readiness and required dependencies.

Aging risk

Days in backlog, promised timing, cancellation exposure and stalled statuses.

Execution priority

Revenue, geography, technician fit and the next action required to move the job.
