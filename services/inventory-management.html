The operating problem

Inventory becomes expensive when purchasing, job demand and warehouse activity are disconnected. Arvenix is designed to create one view of inventory position, committed demand, open orders and replenishment risk.

Inventory Position

On hand, allocated, available, damaged, returned and inbound inventory by item and location.

Demand Signals

Historical usage, sold backlog, scheduled work, seasonality and recent consumption velocity.

Working Capital

Inventory turns, excess stock, stranded material and cash tied up in inventory.
