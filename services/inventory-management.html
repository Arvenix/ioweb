The operating problem

Inventory becomes expensive when materials are purchased without a clear link to demand. Arvenix is designed to connect stock position, sold backlog, scheduled work, purchasing and warehouse activity so operators can make inventory decisions from one operating picture.

Inventory position

On hand, allocated, available, damaged, returned and inbound inventory by SKU and warehouse.

Demand signals

Historical usage, sold backlog, scheduled jobs, seasonality and recent consumption velocity.

Purchasing exposure

Open POs, supplier lead times, duplicate ordering, shortages and excess positions.
